Research

Mustard and fenugreek plants that heard Raga Bhairav in the morning and Raga Yaman Kalyan in the evening germinated and grew better than plants without music, with more chlorophyll, less of the stress marker hydrogen peroxide, and stronger protective enzymes.

Vir, Chauhan, Kumar, Saini & Saini (2026), Impact of Indian classical raga on plant growth and biochemical parameters in Rai and Methi. International Journal of Applied Research 12(5), 281–288. Read the paper

What farmers notice

Research

In a 60-day study with sixty dairy cows, the cows that heard music gave significantly more milk each day than cows without it, and those that heard raga music showed lower levels of the stress hormone cortisol.

Cao, Zhao, Feng et al. (2025), Effects of Raga music and Chinese five-element music on milk production, antioxidant, neuroendocrine, immune, and welfare indicators in dairy cows. Frontiers in Veterinary Science 12, 1623026. Read the paper

About Maharishi Gandharva Veda

What is Maharishi Gandharva Veda?

Gandharva Veda is the classical music of the ancient Vedic civilization of India, which enjoyed Heaven on Earth.

Gandharva Veda music is the eternal music of nature, the rhythms and melodies of nature expressed as music. Gandharva ragas reflect the pure, coherent flow of sound to neutralize stress and disharmony, and gently restore life in tune with the laws of nature.

Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, founder of the Transcendental Meditation programme, has revived Gandharva Veda in its purity by reconnecting it to its source in Veda (Vedic Science, the science of total knowledge).

Music in Tune with the Rhythms of Nature

Gandharva Veda melodies express the rhythms and frequencies of Nature as they change throughout the day and night.

Different Gandharva ragas capture and magnify these frequencies to maximize their benefit to human life. RadyoHarmoni presents Gandharva ragas in their pure form, in tune with these changing values of Nature.

Play Maharishi Gandharva Veda music 24/7, continuously, and contribute to creating world peace. You will enjoy its individual benefits when you listen, but even when no one listens to the melodies, the benefits will spread in the environment, creating balance, peace and harmony in the whole world.

Benefits to Life: Bliss. Peace. Harmony

Those who listen to Gandharva Veda melodies report more bliss, calmness, peace and wellbeing in life.

Research indicates that Gandharva Veda music leads to significant decreases in negative emotions and increases in positive emotions. According to Maharishi’s Festival of Music for World Peace (1986), when Gandharva music was played extensively around the world, negative tendencies in society were notably reduced and replaced by trends of positivity and peace.
