rain-loop.mp3 / rain-loop.m4a - source and processing
Date: 2026-10-19

SOURCE
  The owner's Canva design "Background Editing Request" (revision list v3, row 54), page 5
  "Yağmur sesi dosyası": the sound of the rain video on that page (AAC, 48 kHz stereo, 12.58 s),
  supplied by the owner for the site.

WHAT WAS DONE
  1. Resampled to 44.1 kHz stereo.
  2. Seamless loop: 497,664 samples (11.285 s, a whole number of MP3 and AAC frames). The last 1.30 s
     are crossfaded (equal power) into the first 1.30 s, so the end runs straight into the start.
  3. One fixed gain (+2.2 dB) to about -26 LUFS integrated, as the site's rain level expects. No EQ,
     no compression.
  4. rain-loop.mp3: LAME 112 kbps with gapless header; rain-loop.m4a: Apple AAC 96 kbps (Safari).
  Both decode to exactly 497,664 samples.

Previous loop (until 2026-10-19): Freesound 648529 "RAIN on glass window.wav" by nicoproson (CC0).
