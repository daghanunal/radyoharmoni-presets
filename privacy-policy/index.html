1. What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In short: we collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us.

Personal information provided by you. When you use the contact, photo, video or subscribe forms, we collect what you enter: your first and last name, your email address, your message, the place and link of any photos or videos you share, and whether you would like to receive updates.

Sensitive information. We do not process sensitive information.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes.

Information automatically collected

In short: some information, such as your IP address and browser characteristics, is collected automatically when you visit our Services.

When you visit the Services, our servers automatically record standard log information: IP address, browser type, the pages and streams requested, and date and time stamps. This information does not reveal your specific identity.

If you allow statistics, Google Analytics also records how the website is used: pages viewed, which station you play and for how long, the choices you make on the page, your approximate location (country and city, derived from your IP address), and your device and browser type. See section 5.

The radio uses your device’s time zone to play the raga for your hour. The time zone is read in your browser and is not stored by us; the stream your browser requests reflects only the difference between your time zone and ours. We do not collect location data.

4. When and with whom do we share your personal information?

In short: we may share information in specific situations and with the following third parties.

  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or similar transaction.
  • Our hosting provider: the website and the music streams run on our own servers, hosted by Contabo GmbH in Germany.
  • Statistics: Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, if you allow it (section 5). Google may process this data in the United States.
  • Email delivery: messages you send through the forms reach us by email, delivered through AhaSend, an email delivery service. If you ask for updates, your name and email address are kept in a list on our own server.
  • Spam protection: when you start filling in a form, Google reCAPTCHA v3 is loaded to tell people from automated programs. It sends information about your device and how you use the page to Google. The Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
  • Weather: to show today’s weather in Monte Verde, your browser requests a forecast directly from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). This request includes your IP address, as every web request does, but no other information about you.

Links to other websites (for example concert registration, donations, research papers, or the Veda App) take you to services with their own privacy policies.

5. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

In short: only for statistics, and only as you choose. There is no advertising and no tracking for advertising.

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and to understand how the radio is used, so we can improve it. When you allow statistics, it sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*, which keep a random identifier for up to two years. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Advertising features and Google signals are turned off.

Your choice. On your first visit, a small card asks whether we may count your visit. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Türkiye, nothing is stored until you choose “Allow”. Elsewhere, statistics are on unless you choose “No thanks”. If you decline, Google Analytics sets no cookies; it may still receive a signal without any identifier, so that visits can be counted in total. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices at the bottom of the home page, or use Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Preferences on your device. To remember your choices between visits, the website stores a few settings in your browser’s local storage: the volume, the light or sky you chose, your statistics choice, and whether you have seen a short hint. These stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

reCAPTCHA. If you use one of the forms, Google reCAPTCHA may set its own cookie to protect the form from abuse (see section 4).

The website can also be installed as an app. To make that possible, your browser keeps a copy of the page’s files on your device.
